Expensiq Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: Expensiq keeps your money records on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data. There is no Expensiq account, no bank login and no analytics. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your data goes to your own Google Drive, never to us.

What Expensiq stores

The expenses and income you enter, your categories, payment methods, budgets, recurring bills, and your app settings (such as currency and theme). All of this is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Expensiq has no user accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising. It never connects to your bank, cards or payment apps, and it does not read your SMS messages. If you turn on Payment suggestions (below), it reads payment apps' notifications on your phone, and nothing else. The app does not need an internet connection to work; it only goes online if you turn on Google Drive backup.

Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders or the daily reminder, Expensiq asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

Payment suggestions (optional)

You can allow Expensiq to suggest entries from payment notifications. It is off unless you turn it on in Settings → Payment suggestions, which opens Android's notification access screen.

  • What Expensiq reads: the title and text of notifications from payment and bank apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED and your bank's app). Notifications from every other app, including SMS and messaging apps, are ignored the moment they arrive.
  • What happens to it: the amount and the name in it are shown to you as a suggestion on the Home screen. Nothing is saved as an entry unless you add it. The last 50 payment notifications are kept on your phone only, and each is deleted when you add or dismiss it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Turning it off: Settings → Payment suggestions opens the same Android screen, where you can turn access off at any time.

Backups and exports

When you choose Export backup or Export as spreadsheet, Expensiq creates a file and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where that file goes (for example Google Drive, your Files app, or email). Where you send it is governed by that service's own privacy policy. When you restore a backup, Expensiq only reads the file you pick.

Google Drive backup (optional)

You can choose to sign in with Google so Expensiq backs up your data to your own Google Drive. It is off unless you turn it on.

  • What Expensiq accesses: only a private, hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Expensiq cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files. It also reads your Google account's email address, only to show you which account the backup uses.
  • What it stores there: one backup file with your entries, categories, payment methods, budgets, recurring items and currency, the same data as an exported backup.
  • How it's used: only to save your backup and to restore it when you ask, for example on a new phone. The data goes directly between your phone and Google; it never passes through or is stored on any Expensiq server, and we never see it.
  • Sharing: we do not share, sell or transfer this data to anyone, and do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Turning it off: in Settings → Backup and restore you can turn backup off, which removes Expensiq's access to your Google account. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backup file itself, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for Expensiq.

Expensiq's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling Expensiq or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you used Google Drive backup, see above for how to delete the backup from your Drive.

Children

Expensiq does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Expensiq adds features that handle data differently (for example optional paid features), we will update this page before those features are released and change the date above.
